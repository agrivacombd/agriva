# Plant-science review batch 02 — specimen and distribution evidence

Date: 2026-11-11

This is an evidence supplement. It does not automatically promote every record to fully verified status.

## Bangladesh National Herbarium specimen evidence

### Selaginella tenuissima

BNH's live Plant Specimen Database lists two taxon-level specimen records:

- Accession **65732**, collector Joyanta Chardra Roy, Raziur Rahman & Kamrul Islam; collection no. JCR-375; **2 September 2016**; Kaindya Egojyachari VDG, Balukhali, Rangamati Sadar, Rangamati, Bangladesh.
- Accession **65733**, collector Joyanta Chardra Roy, Kamrul Islam & Suborno Chakma; collection no. JCR-582; **2 October 2016**; Ram Pahar Reserve Forest, Kaptai, Rangamati, Bangladesh.

Decision: specimen-backed Bangladesh occurrence is supported. Keep the catalogue's accepted-name reconciliation as a separate POWO/WCVP check.

### Psilotum nudum

BNH's live database lists multiple taxon-level records:

- Accession **23866**, collector Nazrul Islam; **3 June 1976**; Bagerhat, Khulna, Bangladesh.
- Accession **87173**, collector Dr. M. Sultana; collection no. DMS-4628; **25 May 2022**; Tengragiri Wildlife Sanctuary, Taltoli, Barguna, Bangladesh.
- Accession **86398**, collector Dr. M. Sultana; collection no. DMS-4628; **25 May 2022**; Nishanbaria Beat, Taltoli, Barguna, Bangladesh.

Decision: specimen-backed Bangladesh occurrence is supported. Accession 87173 and 86398 share a collection number/date but different locality labels; retain as separate database records and do not deduplicate without checking herbarium sheets.

### Equisetum diffusum

BNH's Equisetaceae family page lists accession **86599** as *Equisetum diffusum*, but its displayed collection date is the database placeholder `30-11--0001`, collector and locality are blank, and the location field only says Bangladesh.

Decision: this is a taxon-level database entry but weak locality/collection metadata. Do not use it as a georeferenced specimen claim. Cross-check against the cited Bangladesh National Herbarium Bulletin checklist voucher N3113 before upgrading the evidence status.

## POWO taxonomy and distribution findings

### Solanum betaceum Cav.

Kew POWO identifies *Solanum betaceum* as an accepted species in Solanaceae and lists Bangladesh under **introduced** distribution.

Decision: accepted-name status and introduced distribution are supported by POWO. This is not evidence of a native Bangladesh population or a specific BNH voucher. Preserve the introduced qualifier.

### Capsicum pubescens Ruiz & Pav.

Kew POWO identifies *Capsicum pubescens* as an accepted species in Solanaceae, with native range Peru to Bolivia. Bangladesh is not listed in the species-level introduced distribution shown on the page.

Decision: accepted-name status is supported; Bangladesh occurrence is not established by this POWO species page. Do not infer species occurrence from the genus-level *Capsicum* page, which lists Bangladesh at genus level.
