## Interpretation and publication rules

1. These individual POWO pages support accepted taxon concepts for the listed names. They do not validate Bangladesh presence.
2. Only *Solanum betaceum* has Bangladesh explicitly listed on the opened species-level POWO page, and its status is **introduced**.
3. The other listed Solanum and Capsicum pages show native ranges outside Bangladesh and do not list Bangladesh in the displayed introduced ranges. This is not proof of absolute absence; it means the consulted POWO species pages do not substantiate Bangladesh occurrence.
4. Do not infer a species occurrence from a genus-level distribution page.
5. Keep the original catalogue string, the accepted POWO name, and Bangladesh occurrence evidence in separate fields. Do not silently rewrite source strings or promote these rows to fully reviewed.
6. The queue remains `needs_manual_review` until a Bangladesh checklist entry or specimen citation is captured for each row.
