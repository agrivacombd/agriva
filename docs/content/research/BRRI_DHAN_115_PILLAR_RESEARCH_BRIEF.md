## 4. Important discrepancies and claim restrictions

### Amylose content
- BRRI official fact sheet S1: 20%.
- The Daily Star article: 23% (https://online-d11.thedailystar.net/business/news/six-new-rice-varieties-approved-nationwide-cultivation-4100106).
- Action: use 20% with explicit attribution to BRRI's fact sheet. Seek clarification or another primary BRRI document before stating a final value without caveat.

### Health and nutrition claims
BRRI fact sheet contains broad statements about vitamin E and health. AGRIVA should not convert nutrient presence into disease prevention/treatment promises. Do not say the rice cures/prevents cancer, diabetes, heart disease, infertility, or improves immunity unless a suitable human clinical evidence source supports that exact claim. Keep the article to nutrient composition and cautious, general food-nutrition explanation.

### Yield and profitability
- 7.4 t/ha is BRRI's average yield figure in the fact sheet; 8.60 t/ha is described as achievable with suitable care.
- Neither is a guaranteed farmer outcome.
- Do not convert the local market price of other black rice varieties into a BRRI dhan115 farm-gate price.
- No cost/profit figure until local production cost and buyer price are collected with date, location and unit.

### Seed availability
BRRI's seed management portal has a circular-based process, but the checked notice board had no published notice. Do not write “seed is available now”. Give readers a verification path instead.
