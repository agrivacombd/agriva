### GSC query/page overlap observed

For the last 3 months (23 Aug–23 Nov 2026), two black-rice purchase queries were seen on both the existing article and the product page:

| Query | Existing article | Product page |
|---|---|---|
| black rice seed price in bangladesh | 20 impressions, 3 clicks, avg pos 7.2 | 1 impression, 1 click, avg pos 29 |
| কালো ধানের বীজ | 11 impressions, 1 click, avg pos 6.7 | 1 impression, 0 clicks, avg pos 10 |

This is a small observed overlap, not proof of harmful cannibalization. **Keep the article as the informational/buyer-guide owner** (price, unit, seed-checking, purchase guidance) and the product page as the transaction owner (specific product, package, stock, checkout). Do not create a second black-rice-price article; contextual internal linking should clarify the relationship.

Tomato and cucumber buying-guide query demand is **not yet proven by AGRIVA GSC**. The new drafts are hypotheses; keyword/SERP validation remains a publication gate.

## Rules before CMS submission

- Verify current price, stock, package quantity and specs again; prices and stock are volatile.
- Product details must match the current product page/packet label, not be inferred.
- Existing page is updated rather than duplicated where search intent already has an owner.
- Each new guide links to one or more live products, plus relevant AGRIVA cultivation/troubleshooting guide.
- Draft remains unpublished until human editorial/agronomic review, complete CMS inventory reconciliation, keyword/SERP validation, and explicit publication approval.
- Add verified citations/official source references for agronomic claims; this pilot avoids prescriptive fertilizer/pesticide advice.
- Track GSC page/query performance, product-page clicks, and marketplace conversion events at 28/56/90 days after publication.
