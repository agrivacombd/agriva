## Important limitations / unresolved records

### Triticale — row 464

The opened CVPB Poaceae checklist does not contain a matching `Triticosecale` entry. This is a bounded page-level check, not proof that triticale is absent from Bangladesh: cultivated crop records may be held in agricultural trial, seed, or germplasm sources outside the national wild/vascular checklist. No Bangladesh occurrence claim is made.

### Sugar beet and fodder beet — rows 775–776

No individual CVPB Amaranthaceae entry, Bangladesh specimen citation, or Bangladesh-specific crop trial record was captured in this pass for the Altissima or Crassa horticultural groups. POWO's introduced-range entry for the parent species remains global distribution evidence only. Both group-level Bangladesh claims remain unverified.

### Sweet corn / bell pepper / sweet sorghum

CVPB's entries are at the parent-species level. They cannot be copied as evidence for the narrower crop-group descriptors. A cultivar registry, Bangladesh agricultural trial report, seed catalogue with provenance, or specimen record explicitly identifying the crop group is still needed.

## Decisions and publication gate

- Parent-species Bangladesh checklist support: `Zea mays`, `Capsicum annuum`, and `Sorghum bicolor`.
- Bangladesh source support for local `Citrus reticulata` concept and synonym usage: yes; support for the exact catalogue hybrid formula: no.
- No evidence in this batch establishes `Triticosecale`, sugar beet Altissima Group, or fodder beet Crassa Group in Bangladesh.
- Do not promote any queue record to fully verified. Keep verbatim catalogue strings, accepted-name mappings, crop-group concepts, and Bangladesh occurrence in separate fields.
