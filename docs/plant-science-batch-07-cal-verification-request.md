Dear Scientist-E & Head of Office,

I am preparing a botanical data-quality review concerning 12 Cyperaceae names reported as additions to the flora of Bangladesh in the following publication:

Islam, Tamanna Binte & Sarder Nasir Uddin. 2023. “Twelve New Additions to Cyperaceae of Bangladesh.” *Bulletin of Bangladesh National Herbarium* 9: 71–82.

The paper reports examination of 196 CAL herbarium sheets and provides the accession and collection details listed below. We would be grateful if the Central National Herbarium could help us verify, where records are available:

1. Whether each cited CAL accession corresponds to the stated taxon and collector/collection details;
2. The label's verbatim locality, date, collector and collection number;
3. Any later determination, annotation, redetermination or taxonomic note on the sheet;
4. Whether a digital label transcription or catalogue record can be shared for research citation;
5. The procedure, fees (if any), and permitted terms for consulting specimen images for non-commercial academic research.

We understand that specimen images are copyrighted by the Director, Botanical Survey of India, and that the standard image terms restrict website sharing. We are **not** requesting permission to republish images on a website through this enquiry. If any separate permission is required for a specific use, please advise the applicable process.
